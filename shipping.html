Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about delivery and our returns process.

Shipping

We ship across the US and internationally to Canada, the UK, Australia, Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, Germany, France, Netherlands, Ireland and other selected countries. Delivery charges, where applicable, are communicated separately by the business.

Delivery times are estimates. Yehonam Suds is not responsible for delays caused by customs authorities. The recipient is responsible for any import duties, taxes or customs fees applicable in their country.

Order Processing

Orders are processed Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed before 2pm are typically dispatched the same working day. Orders placed after 2pm or over weekends are dispatched the following working day.

Returns Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy, here is how returns work:

  • Returns are accepted within 7 days of opening
  • Products must be unused, unopened and in their original packaging
  • For hygiene reasons, opened personal care products cannot be returned unless they are faulty or not as described
  • Proof of purchase is required for all returns

How to Return an Item

1. Email support@yehonam.com with your order number and the reason for return.
2. We will respond within 1 working day with a returns authorisation and instructions.
3. Pack the item securely in its original packaging.
4. Send the package to the address provided.
5. We recommend using a tracked service. We are not responsible for items lost in return transit.

Faulty or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, faulty or incorrect item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery at support@yehonam.com with your order number and clear photos of the issue. We will arrange a replacement or full refund, including return postage costs, promptly.

Refunds

Once your returned item is received and inspected, we will notify you by email. Approved refunds are processed within 3–5 working days. The time for the refund to appear in your account depends on your bank or card provider — typically 5–10 working days. Any shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Exchanges

We do not currently offer direct exchanges. To exchange a product, return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want.

Contact

For any shipping or returns enquiries, contact us at support@yehonam.com or via our Contact page.
