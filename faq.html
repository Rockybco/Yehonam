Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Yehonam Suds, our products and your orders.

About Our Products

We offer five core categories: Haircare (shampoos, conditioners, treatments, oils), Bodycare (lotions, butters, body wash, scrubs), Skincare (cleansers, moisturisers, serums, masks), Baby Care (gentle washes, lotions and oils for infants and young children) and Home Care (household cleaning products). All products are designed to deliver premium results at affordable prices.

Yes. Our formulas are carefully developed to be gentle and effective for most skin types, including sensitive skin. Each product page lists the full ingredients. If you have a known allergy or specific skin condition, please review the ingredients before purchasing or contact us for guidance.

Our Baby Care range is specifically formulated to be gentle, mild and free from harsh chemicals, making it suitable for newborns and young children. For other product ranges, please check individual product descriptions. When in doubt, consult your healthcare provider.

We are committed to ethical practices. Please refer to individual product packaging or contact us at support@yehonam.com for specific information on our testing standards.

Orders & Payment

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay and other payment methods available through Stripe. All transactions are processed securely using industry-standard encryption. We never store card details on our website.

Yes. All payments are handled by Stripe, one of the world's most trusted payment processors. Your card details are encrypted and processed directly by Stripe. We never see or store your full card information.

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placing them, before fulfilment begins. Contact us immediately at support@yehonam.com or via WhatsApp. Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled.

Yes. A confirmation email is sent to your registered email address as soon as your payment is successfully processed. If you don't receive it within 15 minutes, check your spam folder or contact us.

Shipping & Delivery

Standard UK delivery takes 3–5 working days. Express delivery is 1–2 working days. International orders typically take 7–14 working days depending on the destination country. All timings are estimates and may vary during busy periods.

Yes. Standard shipping is free on all UK orders over £50. Orders below £50 attract a standard shipping fee calculated at checkout.

Yes. We ship to the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, Germany, France, Netherlands, Ireland and many other countries. International shipping costs are calculated at checkout. Some restrictions may apply to certain destinations.

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number. Use the tracking number on the carrier's website to monitor your delivery status.

Returns & Refunds

We accept returns of unused, unopened products in original packaging within 30 days of purchase. For hygiene and safety reasons, opened personal care products cannot be returned unless they are faulty or damaged. See our full Shipping & Returns policy for details.

Contact us within 48 hours of receiving your order at support@yehonam.com with your order number and clear photos of the issue. We will arrange a replacement or full refund promptly.

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, refunds are processed within 3–5 working days. The time for the refund to appear in your account depends on your bank or card provider — typically 5–10 working days.

Customer Support

Email us at support@yehonam.com or message us on WhatsApp using the button on this page. Our support team is available Monday–Friday 9am–6pm and Saturday 10am–3pm. We aim to respond within 24 hours.

Yes. We welcome wholesale and trade enquiries from salons, spas, retailers and businesses. Contact us at support@yehonam.com with your business details and requirements and we will respond with our wholesale pricing and terms.
