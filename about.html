Who We Are

A Brand Built on Passion & Purpose

Yehonam Suds, Hair & Body is a beauty and personal care company established with a passion for promoting healthy hair, radiant skin and quality self-care for the entire family.

Driven by a commitment to quality, innovation and customer satisfaction, the brand focuses on creating products that combine beauty, nourishment and affordability. We believe luxurious self-care should be within reach for everyone.

Our vision is to become a trusted brand offering premium haircare, skincare, baby care and household cleaning products — products that families can rely on every single day.

Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Philosophy

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, affordable and luxurious haircare, skincare and bodycare products that nourish, protect and enhance the natural beauty of individuals and families of all ages.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognised beauty and personal care brand, celebrated for excellence, innovation, quality and trust — while making luxurious self-care accessible to everyone.

Our Philosophy

Everyone deserves to feel beautiful, confident and cared for. Quality personal care is not a luxury — it is a right. We build products that honour that belief every day.

Meet the Founder

Georgina

Founder & CEO

"My passion is to inspire confidence, promote self-care and make quality personal care accessible to everyone — because every person deserves to feel their most beautiful self."

Georgina is the visionary founder and CEO of Yehonam Suds, Hair & Body. With a deep belief in the transformative power of quality personal care, she built Yehonam Suds from the ground up — driven by a desire to serve families with products that genuinely deliver results.

Her commitment to affordability, quality and innovation continues to guide every product decision at Yehonam Suds, ensuring the brand stays true to its core promise: Beauty, Care & Confidence in Every Drop.
